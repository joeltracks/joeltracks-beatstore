# JT focused refinement

November 18, 2026. Guided by Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, https://github.com/heydamir/unslop (CC BY 4.0).

The user approved the focused refinement across customer and seller views, retaining the logo, fonts, palette, features, section order and license terms. Scope is polish, not rebranding or new imagery.

## Direction

- Product: JT helps artists find beats and compare licenses for their next song.
- Character: rhythmic, direct, assured.
- World: recording sessions, track lists and record sleeves.
- Moments: featured beat artwork and the seller's primary data chart.
- Signature: a consistent mint cue for the active beat and playback progress.

## Audit

- Mobile store: introduction, catalog heading, featured beat and expanded filter controls place the first track almost beneath the persistent player at 390×844. Compact grouping and a mobile tags/sort disclosure can expose the list earlier while keeping all controls.
- Store: repeated discovery headings and two cart summaries compete with the title, listening and license actions. Quiet supporting labels and shorten redundant copy.
- Store/license/dashboard: bold price, heading and figure treatments compete. Use 500 for supporting titles and metrics; reserve600 for key titles/emphasis. Keep the selected typefaces.
- Seller sidebar: 35-degree disc rotation and an enlarged analytics icon overstate navigation feedback. Retain a restrained hover response and active marker.
- Motion: rows, cards and footer columns all reveal independently, and the artwork moves/scales with scrolling. Retain useful cart, count-up, chart and page feedback; reduce content entrances to one short route entry and remove decorative artwork travel.
- Dashboard: the Revenue toggle leaves the heading Listening activity. Use a heading that matches the selected data.
- Footer: a full-width green wash adds decorative color away from the cover/data. Use an existing neutral surface; retain mint for meaningful links and controls.

The product already has a consistent identity, real beat controls, a flat logo, line icons, clearly labeled demo data and explicit license review. No hero image, replacement brand, invented metric, legal change or backend work is required.

## Nine-pass implementation plan

1. Spacing: compact catalog groups with 12/16/24/32px steps; retain 44px touch controls. Add4px-based tokens alongside measured source values, preserving the historical extraction. Tighten seller title/stat spacing without changing the shell structure.
2. Color: use existing neutral surfaces; lower secondary cart/label emphasis. Keep black, charcoal, navy seller surfaces and mint. No new palette.
3. Icons: keep the measured1.65-stroke JT family; align common control boxes and simplify hover rotation.
4. Copy: shorten discovery labels, remove short-heading periods and correct the metric-dependent chart heading. Do not edit contract wording, prices, file packages or splits.
5. Type: retain Poppins, DM Sans, Bricolage and the outlined logo. Use fewer weight changes, tabular figures, readable row titles and quiet metadata.
6. Layout: keep sections/order. On mobile, preserve search and category controls while tags/sort remain available through an explicit disclosure. Preserve its state when rerendering or resizing.
7. Details: retain distinct keyboard, active-beat and in-cart states; avoid alignment shifts; keep the contained seller sidebar.
8. Visuals: keep existing covers and charts, with no new generated image. Make the main data trend lead.
9. Motion: one restrained route entry; retain useful chart draw, count-ups, cart drop, menu/dialog and list/detail transitions with reduced-motion support. Preserve adaptive floating boundaries requested earlier.
