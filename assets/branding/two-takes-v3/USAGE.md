# JT / Joel Tracks — Space Grotesk lettering

Updated November 18, 2026 in response to the request for a different logo font.

The approved Two Takes symbol is unchanged. The name uses title-case Space Grotesk at weight 650, converted to SVG paths with optical spacing. The broader, familiar letterforms replace the narrow custom capitals. No font download is needed to display the logo.

Use `jt-horizontal.svg` on the dark customer and seller surfaces; `jt-horizontal-dark.svg` is the light-background variant. Do not stretch the artwork. Website widths are 240px desktop, 190px phone and 174px at 360px or narrower. The footer uses 180px. Existing symbol-only and favicon assets remain in `../two-takes-v2/`.

Font source: [official Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk). The source font and SIL Open Font License are preserved in `../../fonts/space-grotesk-logo-source.ttf` and `../../fonts/space-grotesk-OFL.txt`. License checked November 18, 2026. The earlier logo iterations remain in their original folders.
