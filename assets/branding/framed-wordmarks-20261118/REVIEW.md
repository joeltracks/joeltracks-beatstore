# Joel Tracks — five framed wordmarks

November 18, 2026. Follow-up exploration using the installed logo-design skill.

The seller requested five additional logos with stylistic boundaries and no JT initials. These concepts contain the full name only. “Boundaries” is interpreted as a frame, bracket or enclosure around the lettering. The current website logo has not been replaced; selection is pending.

## Five directions

| Choice | Name | Construction | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Open Frame | Four interrupted corner brackets with alternating lengths; optically spaced Space Grotesk 560 | Strongest general header readability; restrained character |
| B | Soft Seal | Rounded capsule with soft Fraunces 650 lettering cut out as transparent contours | Strong silhouette; uses more solid area |
| C | Session Label | Stepped perimeter and condensed Barlow Bold capitals | Compact label character; capitals are smaller at equal header height |
| D | Orbit | Tapered, asymmetric open oval and Yellowtail full-name script | Most personal and expressive; needs more width for small uses |
| E | Index | One raised tab and rounded lower corners, with Bricolage 650 lettering | A distinctive perimeter; a more utilitarian character |

A is recommended for everyday website use. D is the alternative for a stronger producer-signature identity. The seller chooses the direction.

## Brief and exploration

Audience: independent artists buying R&B, Afro and Trap beats. Character: assured, readable, rhythmic. Primary use: the existing dark storefront header. All concepts are tested in monochrome and reversed on dark, rather than selected through color preference.

Ten ideas were considered: open corner brackets, a capsule, a stepped label, an open oval, a raised tab, nested rectangles, a circular seal, a folded ribbon, a serrated stamp and a waveform boundary. The first five offer distinct enclosures while keeping the full name readable. The remaining directions add detail, reduce useful type width or introduce category clichés. No monograms, initials, music-note glyphs or copied logos were used.

## Verification and iterations

The five v1 drawings were rendered and visually inspected. The v2 pass fixed overlapping contours in the capsule knockout through a vector difference operation and adjusted word spacing in B, D and E. The final v2 comparison was rendered and inspected again, including all five reversed previews at an actual 48px logo height.

All five logo masters are outlined SVG paths with one flat color, no live text, raster, filters or gradients. B's lettering is transparent negative space, not white paint. The board's captions use live presentation text only. The structural audit was run twice. Remaining audit advisories concern complexity and natural angles in the font outlines; a zero-warning audit is not claimed. Borders use deliberate orthogonal geometry or smooth curves. All five names read in the header previews; C/D are less suitable for very narrow placements. Full-name wordmarks are not claimed readable as 16px favicons, and no initials-based icon was introduced.

Files: `five-framed-wordmarks-v2.png` and `.svg` are the latest board. `a-open-frame-v2.svg` through `e-index-v2.svg` are the five masters. Earlier v1 files remain for comparison. No final kit or website implementation was added.
