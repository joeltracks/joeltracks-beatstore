# JT / Joel Tracks — Two Takes

Approved direction C. Two offset track corners share an open center. The abstract symbol is paired with the name in the storefront and studio so customers can identify Joel Tracks immediately.

## Masters and variants

- `jt-symbol.svg` is the primary charcoal symbol on a 256-unit canvas.
- `jt-horizontal.svg` is the primary name-and-symbol lockup used on the website. `jt-stacked.svg` and `jt-wordmark.svg` cover alternate formats. All lettering is outlined and requires no installed font.
- `jt-symbol-reversed.svg` and `jt-horizontal-reversed.svg` use light ink on a dark background. Symbol strokes are slightly thinned for optical balance.
- `jt-symbol-small.svg` is the pixel-aligned 16px cut. The tiny horizontal strokes are simplified to two pixels.
- `lockups/` contains black, white and burnt-orange horizontal and stacked exports. `web/` contains symbol variants, the favicon ICO and PNG icon set, touch icon and optional manifest. The website uses `assets/jt-mark.svg` as its tiled SVG favicon.
- `presentation.html` shows six possible applications. These mockups are illustrations of the identity, not merchandise or extra site features. `tests.html` contains scale and background checks.

The palette retains the website's current colors. Prefer charcoal on light backgrounds and paper on charcoal. Orange is an optional supporting mark color. Print CMYK and spot-color values need matching against the actual printer and substrate.

The outlined wordmark uses Bricolage Grotesque at weight 600, optical size 24, with adjusted letter spacing. The website continues to use DM Sans for interface text. Their SIL Open Font License files are in `assets/fonts`. The logo artwork does not require those fonts to display.

## Placement

Keep the mark upright. Preserve the fixed relationship of the two corners. Allow at least one stem width of clear space around the visible symbol, equivalent to 36 units on the master canvas. Its 32-unit canvas margin supplies most of this, so allow a further 4 units when placing another object beside the file's edge.

Use the small cut from 16–24px and the standard symbol from 32px. Use the horizontal lockup at 170px wide or larger for normal interface viewing. At smaller sizes use the symbol with an accessible text label. The current website uses 198px desktop and 174px mobile lockups.

Do not stretch, rotate, add outlines, close the center gap or place busy imagery directly behind the logo. Do not animate the two corners independently. Keep the mark steady during browsing.

## Review and limits

The symbol and small cut pass the skill's SVG geometry checks. The name lockups contain more anchors because their letters are outlined. The audit's near-angle flags occur in the original font's e crossbar and are retained to preserve the letterform, rather than applying geometric snapping to type. The stacked canvas was expanded after an overflow check and rechecked.

The logo has been visually checked at 16, 32 and 64px and in the website's light header. Browser checks cover mobile navigation at 320px and the desktop seller header. The mark is original artwork, but this is not trademark clearance. Arrange a professional clearance search before commercial adoption.
